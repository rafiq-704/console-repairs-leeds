Common Faults

USB-C Charging Port

If your Nintendo Switch 2 does not charge, charges intermittently, or only works at certain angles, the USB-C charging port may be damaged. We provide precision port repairs and replacements.

From £50

No Video via Dock

If your console works in handheld mode but shows no display when docked, the issue may relate to the USB-C port, dock connector, or HDMI circuitry.

From £50

Joy-Con Drift / Rail Issues

Movement without touching the joystick or controllers disconnecting frequently is a common issue. We replace faulty joystick modules and repair rail connectors.

From £40

Overheating

Overheating can cause crashes, loud fan noise, and performance issues. We clean internal components and replace thermal materials to restore cooling efficiency.

From £40

Battery Replacement

Poor battery life, swelling, or sudden shutdowns indicate battery wear. We install high-quality replacement batteries for reliable long play sessions.

Wi-Fi Connectivity Issues
