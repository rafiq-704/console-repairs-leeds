Sony PSP

The Sony PlayStation Portable (PSP) remains a classic handheld console, beloved for its multimedia features, strong performance, and extensive game library. Despite its age, many gamers continue to use and collect the PSP, which is why we provide fast, reliable repairs across all models. Our expert technicians can resolve the majority of faults that occur, ensuring your PSP stays in excellent condition.

Being a portable console, the PSP can develop faults over time. Common issues include **screen damage, unresponsive buttons, UMD drive faults, charging or battery problems, power failures, and speaker or sound issues**. Our fully equipped workshop handles all these problems and many more not listed here.

Common Faults

UMD Drive Repairs

If your PSP fails to read UMD discs or makes unusual noises, the UMD drive may be faulty. Our technicians repair or replace UMD mechanisms to restore game loading.

Screen & Casing Repairs

Cracked screens, broken casings, or snapped hinges are common PSP issues. We replace damaged screens and repair housing to restore appearance and usability.

From £30

Button & Speaker Faults

Unresponsive buttons, faulty controls, or speaker issues can affect gameplay. We repair or replace internal components to restore full functionality.
