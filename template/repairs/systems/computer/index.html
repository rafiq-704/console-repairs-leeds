Computer Repairs

COMPUTER REPAIR LEEDS

We repair all brands of PC, including Apple, Samsung, DELL, Sony Vaio and Lenovo

We take a proactive and preventative approach to PC repairs. Rather than simply fixing the immediate issue, we focus on long-term system health. If your computer is running slowly due to a virus or malware infection, we do more than just remove the threat—we take comprehensive steps to minimise the risk of reinfection. This includes applying all manufacturer-released security updates, strengthening system settings, and disabling unused or potentially vulnerable applications.

We also provide clear, honest advice on antivirus software—highlighting which solutions we recommend, which we avoid, and the reasons behind those decisions. Our team includes technicians with deep expertise in computer security. In fact, the author of this page has worked within the computer security field for decades, and the policies and procedures we use today to protect our customers’ systems have been developed and refined over many years of hands-on experience.

While virus removal and software troubleshooting form a significant part of our work, they are far from the only services we provide. We offer comprehensive hardware repair solutions for virtually every fault imaginable, including logic board repairs, processor-related issues, hard-drive upgrades, and a wide range of discrete component replacements. Thanks to our well-equipped workshop and extensive parts inventory, we are often able to complete repairs the very same day—frequently within just a few hours.

No Display / Black Screen

If your computer turns on but shows no display, a black screen, or random flickering, it may be caused by a faulty LCD/LED panel, loose display cable, damaged RAM, GPU failure, or motherboard issue. We provide full diagnostics and repair for all display-related faults.

Physical Damage / Broken Parts

Physical damage such as cracked casing, broken hinges, dented frames, damaged ports, or bent laptop bodies can affect both performance and safety. We repair or replace damaged components and restore your device to a stable, fully functional condition.

Keyboard / Touchpad / Button Faults

Issues like unresponsive keys, faulty touchpads, stuck power buttons, or broken volume buttons often result from liquid damage, worn-out circuits, faulty flex cables, or internal hardware failure. We provide precise repairs and genuine replacement parts for all input-related issues.
