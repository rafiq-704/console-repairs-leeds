XBOX X | S

Experiencing issues with your Xbox Series X or Series S? No stress – our certified technicians handle all next-gen repairs from hardware faults to system software recovery. Most repairs are completed within 24–48hrs and include warranty for your peace of mind.

Whether it’s no display, overheating, storage corruption, HDMI port damage, or random crashes, we fix every issue commonly found in the Xbox Series X & S. Our engineers repair at component level using professional micro-soldering tools – meaning fast, safe, and cost-effective solutions without motherboard replacement unless required.

Free Diagnostics – No inspection fee. Approve only if you wish to continue.
Professional Technicians – Experienced in modern consoles, board repairs & firmware.
OEM Parts Only – We use genuine quality parts with warranty coverage.

Common Xbox Series X/S Repairs

HDMI Port / Output Issues

No signal, flickering display, or broken pins? We repair or replace faulty HDMI ports with precision micro-soldering.

From £60

HDMI Retimer / BGA Chip

If the display remains black after port repair, the HDMI retimer or BGA chip may have failed. Professional replacement ensures stable output.

From £70

Overheating / Fan Noise

Loud fans or heat issues? Thermal paste refresh, dust removal, and airflow optimization reduce shutdowns and heat throttling.

From £60

UHD Blu-Ray Drive Faults

Disc reading failures, eject issues, or scratching discs. We repair or replace laser modules for smooth playback.

From £60

Internal SSD / OS Issues

Boot errors, OS corruption, or stuck on green logo. SSD reprogramming, rebuild, or replacement is available.

From £60

Wi-Fi / Network Issues

Connectivity problems? Wi-Fi IC, antenna, or LAN port repairs restore stable online play and downloads.

From £50

No Power / Instant Shutdown

Console won't turn on or instantly powers off? Board-level PSU and internal repairs restore power stability.

From £50

Liquid Damage
